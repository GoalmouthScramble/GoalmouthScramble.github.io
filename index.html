Top story

Dartford's player-manager scores at Lewes on Bonfire night

Ben Greenhalgh picked himself to start at the Dripping Pan, and after 22 minutes he curled the opener into the top corner. Dan Smith doubled the lead seven minutes later, pouncing on the rebound after a save, and although Lewes (4th) pulled one back late on, Dartford (5th) held on to win 2-1. It ended a run of three straight league defeats, and handed Lewes their first home league defeat since the opening day of the season.

Greenhalgh took an unusual route to non-league management. In 2010 he won Sky One's Football's Next Star, and the prize was a contract with Inter Milan. He never played a first-team game there, but he travelled with the squad to that year's Champions League final and was presented with a winner's medal. Now 34, he has Dartford level on points with Lewes in the play-off places.

Dartford's (5th) win at Lewes (4th) pulled them level on points with their hosts in the play-off places. Further up, Eastbourne Borough (3rd) made it eight wins in a row, which leaves them just three points off the top with a game in hand on Welling United (2nd).

As a result, just two points now separate everyone from 6th to 13th. At the bottom, Brentwood Town (19th) lost again and remain in the drop zone, level on points with Cray Wanderers (18th), who have four games in hand on them.

Bad week

Burgess Hill Town (13th)
