Midweek round-up

Eastbourne back up to third

Eastbourne Borough (3rd) beat Aveley (14th) 3-1 on Tuesday for their 12th home win in a row in all competitions, and that took them back above Dartford (4th) into third. They are now level on 38 points with Enfield Town (2nd) and three behind Welling United (1st), who play each other on Saturday.

Leatherhead (7th) beat Ramsgate (5th) 1-0 to climb back into the play-off places, which pushed AFC Whyteleafe (8th) out without kicking a ball. Cray Wanderers (10th) needed an 89th-minute equaliser to draw 1-1 with Wingate & Finchley (9th), so their run of four league wins ended, although they are unbeaten in five. Below the top seven it is as tight as ever, with only five points between the ten clubs from Whyteleafe down to Brentwood Town (17th).

Top story

The Leatherhead curse

Ramsgate (5th) went to Fetcham Grove on Tuesday higher in the table than Leatherhead (7th) and with better records at both ends, and they still lost 1-0. It was the third time the clubs had met this season, and Leatherhead had won all three, including a penalty shootout in the FA Trophy in September. That morning one Ramsgate fan had called Leatherhead "our bogey team", before adding hopefully that "jinxes can be overcome though". By midnight the club's own account was calling it "a disappointing evening in Surrey as the Leatherhead curse continues."

Leatherhead's match writer enjoyed every minute. "If Santa is a Ramsgate fan, he’s very likely giving serious thought to adding Tanners to the Naughty List," began Bryant's Bulletin, which also noticed that Ramsgate's "usually large travelling support decided to stay away". The crowd of 310 was Leatherhead's smallest of the season, and the win lifted them back into the play-off places.

Good week

Misha Djemaili (CM)

Cray Wanderers (10th)

Signed from Hashtag United last week, he scored on his debut against Whitehawk (22nd) on Saturday and set up the other goal. On Tuesday he started on the bench against Wingate & Finchley (9th), then came on with a quarter of an hour left and Cray a goal down. "Mr. Clutch," was one Cray fan's verdict.

Next up is a trip to Welling United (1st), who have won all ten of their home league games.

In other news

Two down, twice

On Saturday Maldon & Tiptree (18th) were 3-1 down at Chatham Town (13th) with seven minutes left and drew 3-3. On Tuesday they went 2-0 down at Buckhurst Hill of the North Division in the Velocity Cup, and their half-time substitutes turned it round. Jamie Bennett put them ahead in the 84th minute, and Callum Maltese equalised two minutes later, only for Bennett to win it in stoppage time in front of a crowd of 75. Their reward is a home tie with Dulwich Hamlet (16th).

Segregated in January

Eastbourne Borough (3rd) have already made their home game against Lewes (6th) on 2 January a segregated fixture, expecting a big crowd for the Sussex derby. Lewes fans get 589 tickets, 492 standing and 97 seated, and the seats cost the same as standing on a first come, first served basis. Tickets went on sale on Wednesday, more than five weeks before kick-off.

Back to 2006 prices
