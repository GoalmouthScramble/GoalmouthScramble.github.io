Weekend round-up

Welling hang on at the top

Welling United (1st) needed Lekan Majoyegbe's header in the sixth minute of stoppage time to draw 2-2 at Dulwich Hamlet (16th). Their lead still stayed at three points, because Enfield Town (2nd) and Eastbourne Borough (4th) shared eight goals at the David Bryant Stadium. Dartford (3rd) made the most of it, as a 3-0 win over Leatherhead (8th) was their fourth league win in a row and took them above Eastbourne into third.

Further down, AFC Whyteleafe (7th) won 4-3 at St Albans City (12th) to climb into the play-off places, and Ramsgate (5th) beat Lewes (6th) 4-1 to swap places with them. Cray Wanderers (11th) also made it four league wins in a row, which leaves them three points off seventh with five games in hand. At the bottom, Three Bridges (20th) came from behind to beat Cheshunt (19th) for their first league win in nine games, and the gap between the two is now five points.

Top story

A full back's clean sheet

Aveley (14th) were 1-0 up at Carshalton Athletic (10th) when their goalkeeper, Jamie Grossart (GK), was hurt late in the first half. After a long delay he was stretchered off, and with no goalkeeper among the substitutes, full back Prosper Keto (FB) went in goal for more than 50 minutes. He held a long-range free kick from the Carshalton captain before the break, then tipped away a close-range shot from substitute Lui Edwards four minutes from time, as Aveley won 2-0.

It was the second league game in a row in which Carshalton had faced an outfield player in goal. In their previous one, Cheshunt (19th) lost goalkeeper R'avan Constable to injury, and Carshalton scored four. This time they failed to score at all.

Good week, bad week, crazy week

Good week

Toby Aromolaran (FWD)

AFC Whyteleafe (7th)

With the score 1-1 at St Albans City (12th), he had a shot saved after Montel McKenzie's run and cross, then followed up to score from the rebound. St Albans fought back to 2-2, but Whyteleafe broke twice in the closing minutes. He finished the first of those counter-attacks in the 86th minute, then turned in a drilled cross from the wing in the fourth minute of stoppage time. The hat-trick took him to 17 league goals, seven more than anyone else in the division, and lifted Whyteleafe into the play-off places.

Bad week

Sam Youngs (FWD)

Enfield Town (2nd)

Enfield's 400-appearance man is chasing the club's scoring record, but in the 23rd minute against Eastbourne Borough (4th) he headed a cross into his own net to put the visitors 2-1 up. He made up for it after the break, running onto goalkeeper Dylan Thompson's long ball and squaring for Luke May-Parrott to equalise. Enfield then went 4-2 up with 19 minutes left. However, Eastbourne scored twice in seven minutes, and he was substituted with the game level at 4-4.

Crazy week

Kai Corbett

Eastbourne Borough (4th)

He came on in the 68th minute at Enfield Town (2nd) with Eastbourne 3-2 down, and three minutes later a deflected cross squirmed under his own goalkeeper to make it 4-2. Then, in the 74th minute, he turned in fellow substitute T'yano Wilson's cross. Seven minutes after that he cut inside and drove a shot into the bottom corner from 20 yards. Two goals in seven minutes off the bench rescued a point and took him to nine for the league season.

Beaten at the parent club

Whitehawk (22nd) started Henry Molyneux (GK) in goal at Flamingo Park, even though he is on loan to them from Cray Wanderers (11th). Twenty years at Princes Park

Dartford's (3rd) ground is 20 years old this month. Leatherhead's (8th) match writer approved of the all-round cover, the big scoreboard, the £2.20 parking and a chips, Mars bar and Bovril combination for £6.70. The pitch sits below ground level by design, to cut light and noise pollution, and the writer wondered whether that explained the state of the goalmouths. Leatherhead lost 3-0 there all the same.
