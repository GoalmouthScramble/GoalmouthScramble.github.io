Midweek round-up

Dartford up to fourth

Visiting sides won three of Tuesday's four league games, but the one home win made the biggest difference near the top. Dartford (4th) beat ten-man Three Bridges (20th) 1-0 for a third straight league win, which lifted them above Lewes (5th). Ramsgate (6th) lost 3-2 at home to Cray Wanderers (14th), who climbed three places and still have games in hand on everyone around them.

The race for the last play-off place has tightened. Wingate & Finchley (8th) won 1-0 at Leatherhead (7th), which left those two and Carshalton Athletic (9th) all on 25 points, with Leatherhead holding seventh on goal difference. At the bottom, Brentwood Town (18th) won 1-0 at Burgess Hill Town (13th) to climb out of the relegation places. That pushed Cheshunt (19th) into them on a night when they were beating Aveley (16th) 4-3 in the Velocity Cup.

Top story

Cray's false position

Nine days after taking Stockport County to extra time in the FA Cup, Cray Wanderers (14th) went to Ramsgate (6th) on Tuesday and won 3-2, having led 3-1 until the final minute. It was their third win in seven days, after beating Three Bridges (20th) 2-0 and Folkestone Invicta 3-1 in the FA Trophy. The club worked out that it was the first time since March 1997 that they had won at Folkestone and Ramsgate in back-to-back away games.

The table still has them 14th, but they have played only 12 league games, six fewer than Welling United (1st). Their 21 points work out at 1.75 a game, and only Enfield Town (2nd), Welling, Eastbourne Borough (3rd) and Dartford (4th) have a better average. On that measure, the club in 14th is the fifth-best side in the division.
