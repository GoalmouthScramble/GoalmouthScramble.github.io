Weekend round-up

Welling go top

Welling United (1st) won 3-1 at St Albans City (8th), and with Enfield Town (2nd) losing at home to AFC Whyteleafe (13th), they go top by two points. Enfield have a game in hand, however, and they host Lewes (4th) on Tuesday. Eastbourne's defeat at Dartford means just six points now cover the top five, with Lewes and Dartford level on 30 after both won.

Further down, Carshalton Athletic (7th) won for the first time in seven league games and moved into the play-off places, which pushed St Albans out of them after a second defeat in five days. At the bottom, Brentwood Town (18th) climbed out of the relegation zone with a point at Three Bridges (20th). That dropped Cray Wanderers (19th) into it without kicking a ball, although they have played five games fewer than Brentwood.

Top story

Steve King's unhappy return to Dartford

On Friday, Steve King was named the division's Manager of the Month for October, after his Eastbourne Borough (3rd) side won all five of their league games. On Saturday he took them back to Dartford (5th), the club he used to manage, where he won the Kent Senior Cup twice and reached a National South play-off final.

By half-time his side were 4-0 down, and although they pulled one back after the break, they lost 4-1. It ended Eastbourne's run of eight straight league wins, at the ground where King's Dartford teams once lost only seven of 44 home league games.

Good week, bad week, crazy week

Good week

Slavomir Huk (GK)

AFC Whyteleafe (13th)

Enfield Town had scored ten goals in their previous two league games, and they were top of the table when Whyteleafe visited on Saturday. With his side 1-0 up, the Slovak kept out Sam Youngs's 57th-minute penalty, his second penalty save in a week and, by the club's count, his fourth of the season. Whyteleafe then scored again late on, knocked the leaders off the top, and named Huk their man of the match.

Bad week

Sam Youngs (FWD)

Enfield Town (2nd)

Enfield's usually reliable penalty taker had the chance to equalise against AFC Whyteleafe (13th) after 57 minutes, only to see Huk save his spot kick. He was substituted five minutes later, and Whyteleafe went on to add a second eight minutes from time. As a result, Enfield lost a league game for the first time since mid-September, and with it top spot.

Crazy week

Liam Nash

Eastbourne Borough (3rd)

He scored five goals in 11 games for Dartford (5th), and he has been out for much of this season with a fractured shoulder. On Saturday he came off the bench at his old club with his side already 4-0 down, and within a minute he had scored. It was the only good news Eastbourne had all afternoon.

In other news

Nine minutes from history

After 78 FA Cup campaigns, Cray Wanderers (19th) finally reached the first round proper on Sunday, as the Isthmian League's last club in the competition. All 2,500 tickets sold out in ten minutes, a new stand was finished in time for the game, and the BBC cameras were there for the first time. With nine minutes left they led League One's Stockport County 2-0 against ten men, only to lose 3-2 after extra time.
